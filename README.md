### 🔬 Research

*Parameter-Efficient Hate Speech Detection in Code-Mixed Hinglish via LoRA*
NLP · Transformer Fine-tuning · LoRA · HingRoBERTa · MuRIL — In Progress, targeting submission to an NLP/computational linguistics venue

- Fine-tuned *HingRoBERTa* and *MuRIL* with LoRA across ranks r ∈ {4, 8, 16, 32} on the *TRAC-1 Hinglish hate speech dataset* (11,091 examples). *HingRoBERTa LoRA at r=4 reached Macro F1 0.704 while training only 0.27% of total parameters.*
- Found a region of parameter-efficiency equivalence: HingRoBERTa LoRA at r=4 and r=8 are statistically indistinguishable from MuRIL full fine-tuning (p=0.99, p=0.66) — evidence that domain-aligned pretraining substantially lowers adaptation cost.
- Identified and characterized a three-phase collapse pattern in MuRIL (collapse → threshold-edge → plateau), jointly gated by LoRA rank and learning rate.
- Group research project — repo to be linked once shared by collaborators.
